## Rewritten (riddle text changed)
- nature-042 cobra: cobras cannot hear the pungi. They follow the pipe's movement, so "sway to their music" became "sway by waving their pipes in front of me".
- nature-062 seahorse: "a head shaped like a tiny horse" gave the name away. Replaced with upright swimming, a gripping tail and bony armour. Stays medium.
- nature-063 termite: the clue contained the alias "white ant", so copying it from the riddle won. The clue now says people mistake it for an ant and its closest relatives are cockroaches. The `white ant` alias is kept because it is the same insect (a common Indian name).
- nature-069 strawberry: botanically, the specks on the outside are achenes, tiny fruits that each hold a seed. They are not bare seeds. The clue now says so. Relabelled medium to easy.
- nature-076 Mars: removed the "Red Planet" giveaway. Stays medium.
- nature-077 Saturn: removed the "rings" giveaway. Now uses second-largest planet, bathtub density and Titan's methane lakes. Stays medium.
- nature-113 nails: "at your fingertips" plus "painted for parties" made it easy. Now uses "twenty of us", rhino-horn keratin, painless trimming and the pale half-moon (lunula). Added `toenails` and `toenail`. Stays hard.
- nature-115 ostrich: "biggest egg" gave it away. Replaced with "two toes per foot", which rules out emu, rhea and cassowary (three toes each). Stays hard.
- nature-121 Venus: "a single day lasts longer than a year" is only true of the sidereal rotation (243 days against a 225-day year). The solar day is about 117 days. Reworded to "one turn on my axis takes longer than my whole trip around the Sun".
- nature-124 horizon: removed the definitional first line ("where the sky meets land or sea"). Now uses ships sinking out of view and the sunrise. Stays hard.
- nature-125 water: "covers most of the planet, your body is half me" made it easy. Now uses its three natural states and ice floating on liquid water. The hint changed from the H2O formula to "Clouds, rivers and glaciers are all made of me." Stays hard.

## Writer's flags: decisions
- **turtle for tortoise (013):** kept. A tortoise is a land turtle, and the fable is widely told as "the turtle and the hare".
- **mist for fog (072):** kept. It is the same thing (water droplets hanging at ground level). The two differ only by a visibility threshold, and `kohra` covers both. The "barely see through me" clue still points to fog as the main answer.
- **air for wind (106):** removed (see above).
- **whale for blue whale (044):** kept. A blue whale is a whale, and someone who types "whale" has solved it. It is not a different animal.
- **fungus / toadstool for mushroom (095):** kept. A mushroom is the fruiting body of a fungus, and "I am not a plant" invites that answer. Toadstool is a common name for a mushroom. Added `fungi`.
- **cuckoo for koel (089):** kept. The Asian koel belongs to the cuckoo family, and people in India commonly call it a cuckoo. The clues (glossy black male, red eyes, eggs laid in crows' nests) fit no other bird.
- **white ant for termite (063):** alias kept, clue rewritten (see above).
- **strawberry seeds outside (069):** fixed (see above).
- **Difficulty of tides / pearl / blood / appendix:** all hard to medium.
- **Crow calling means guests (041):** kept. It is phrased as a belief ("people say"). The hiccup hint (083, "Some say...") is also phrased as a belief.
- **Garlic and vampires (068):** kept. It is phrased as legend ("Legend says").
