## Flagged items reviewed and kept
- `ghadi`/`ghari` in 006 (wall clock) and 007 (wristwatch): the Hindi word covers both objects. The clues separate the two riddles: 006 is on the wall and ticks, 007 has a strap and sits on the wrist.
- `seedhi` in 049 (ladder) and 125 (stairs): Hindi uses one word for both. 049 is leaned against a wall to reach the roof. 125 never moves and the lift took its visitors, and its hint says "unlike a ladder".
- `headphones` in 044 and 091: 044 sits over the ears, so earbuds can't fit it. 091 is two buds with tails that tangle, so over-ear headphones can't fit it. `headphones` stays as an alias on 091 because Indian players commonly use the word for wired earphones. Anyone typing it means that object.
- 063 zipper `chain`: Indian English for a zip (e.g. "pant ki chain"), and no actual chain fits the riddle. Kept.
- 020 charger `adapter`: common Indian usage for the charger brick. A plain travel adapter doesn't "bring the phone back to life". Kept.
- 109 router `wifi`/`modem`: players say "restart the wifi", and the ISP box in Indian homes is a combined modem-router that fits every clue. Kept.
- 045 car `taxi`: a taxi is a car and fits every clue. Kept.
- 023 shoes `boots`: boots also have tongues and laces, so the riddle genuinely fits them. Accepting them is correct.
- 084 microwave `oven`: a common name for a microwave, and a normal oven doesn't spin food or beep. Kept.
- 016 auto-rickshaw meter joke: mild. Kept.
