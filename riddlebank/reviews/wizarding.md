# wizarding.jsonl review

125 riddles checked. Each riddle was solved before its answers were read, and every fact was checked against the books and films.

- Kept unchanged: 107
- Rewritten (riddle text): 14
- Answer or hint fixes only: 4
- Relabelled: 0 (split is 56 easy / 44 medium / 25 hard, the target 45/35/20, and each label looked honest)
- Dropped: 0

JSON is valid, all 125 ids are unique and still in order (001 to 125), riddles are 300 characters or fewer, hints are 100 or fewer, and every answer is lower case and 1 to 3 words.

## Rewrites
- 001 Hedwig: "for his eleventh birthday" read as Hagrid's birthday. Now "the boy's".
- 017 Hufflepuff: "common room close to the kitchens" comes from Pottermore, not the books. Replaced with the jolly friar house ghost.
- 032 Azkaban: "North Sea" is not stated in the books. Now "far out at sea".
- 046 Draco: the robe-fitting first meeting is book-only (in the film they meet on the stairs). Now marked "in the books".
- 058 Errol: the writer flagged it as too vague. Now it is anchored on delivering Ron's Howler at breakfast and crash-landing (true in both book and film).
- 059 Crookshanks: removed "part Kneazle" (outside-canon source). Now uses book facts only: leapt onto Ron's head in the pet shop, went home with Hermione, hunted the rat all year. Marked "in the books".
- 067 Shrieking Shack: "most haunted building in Britain" was too close to the book's wording. Reworded.
- 070 Godric's Hollow: "West Country" comes from outside the books, so it was removed. The memorial turning into a statue is book-only, so it is now marked "In the book".
- 072 Beauxbatons: "as tall as Hagrid" became "roughly as tall as Hagrid" (the book says there was barely an inch in it, and the film also casts her very tall).
- 079 Fat Friar: "a monk in life" was wrong because a friar is not a monk. Removed.
- 081 Bloody Baron: the "gaunt, blank-eyed ... robes stained with silver blood" description was close to the book's words. Reworded, and "chains by choice" became "worn as penance" (from Deathly Hallows).
- 122 Hand of Glory: Draco eyeing it in Borgin and Burkes is book-only, so it is now marked "In the books". The object stays unnamed ("thing").
- 123 S.P.E.W.: "What was it called?" asked for the full society name, but the answer is "spew". Now it asks what word the initials spell.
- 124 Mimbulus mimbletonia: "grey cactus ... boils rather than spines" was close to the book's words. Reworded.

## Answer or hint fixes
- 021 Patronus: removed "patronus charm", because the riddle itself says it means the animal and not the spell.
- 033 Muggle: removed "no maj", "no-maj" and "nomaj", because the riddle asks what British wizards say.
- 069 Grimmauld Place: hint changed from "Kreacher polishes the silver here" (not accurate, since he hoarded more than he cleaned before Deathly Hallows) to "Sirius hated being stuck here."
- 099 Prongs: removed "james" and "james potter", because the riddle asks for the nickname, not the person.

## Flagged items kept as they are
- 006 "cloak": kept. In this riddle a cloak that makes you vanish can only be the Invisibility Cloak.
- 012 Hermione hitting Draco: "hit ... in the face" is true for both the book slap and the film punch.
- 011 Dumbledore as "bumblebee": a real English dialect word, so the fact is true.
- 075 Bludger as "iron": comes from Quidditch Through the Ages, a Rowling canon book.
