# desi.jsonl checker log

125 riddles checked. Each one was solved blind before its answers were read. Every blind answer matched the writer's intended answer, but some clues and aliases needed tightening.

- **Kept:** 98. This includes 10 answers-only fixes and 1 hint-only fix.
- **Rewritten:** 16. One of these (013) was also relabelled.
- **Relabelled only:** 11
- **Dropped:** 0. No gods, rituals or religion comparisons were found. The festival riddles (diya, rangoli, rakhi, gulal, pichkari, dandiya, phuljhadi, anar, mehendi, baraat) are about customs, lights, colours and objects, so they stay.

Final mix: easy 60 (48%) / medium 48 (38%) / hard 17 (14%). Hard is below the ~20% target. Many "hard" riddles were instant for this audience of Indian adults in their 30s and 40s. Where possible the giveaway clue was removed so the riddle could stay hard, and the rest were relabelled honestly.
