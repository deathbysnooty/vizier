Fire & Blood

How the month works

Everything you need, in the order you will need it.

1 · The egg

Anyone who wants in claims an egg. That is the only thing you have to do. It sits with you for a week and watches whatever you were already doing — it needs no feeding and no babysitting.

On 8 October at 12 noon every egg hatches at once, and yours tells you which house you belong to. Nobody picks, nobody is picked.

2 · The one strange rule

During the egg week, your egg is hungry for two games at a time, and only those two pay you. Everything else still runs and is still fun; it just does not feed your egg.

Everyone gets different games. If all of us had the same two, fifty people would pile into anagrams and every other channel would be dead. Over the week your egg will have asked for every game.

Type /egg to see what yours wants right now and when it changes. The warmer your egg, the bigger the dragon you hatch — and ignoring it entirely still hatches one, just a small one.

3 · The hatch, and why the houses will be even

The egg uses your week — how much you actually played — and deals everyone out so the four houses end up equally matched in activity, not in headcount.

One house might have thirty members and another twenty-two and still be dead level. The quiet members are spread out instead of landing in one place. No house is carried by three people, and no house is dead weight. That was September's problem; this is the fix.

4 · Ravens

The Golden Snitch is gone, and so are the frog drops. One thing replaces both: a raven lands carrying a sealed scroll. Anyone can press Capture — everyone who presses gets their own riddle, privately — and the first correct answer takes it.

Inside is a card. Every card is numbered: yours is House Targaryen, No. 016, and nobody else on this server will ever hold that one. Your old frog cards come across with their numbers intact.

5 · Points and limits

One points pile. No currencies. Your points are your dragon's size — that is the whole scoreboard.

  • Quick games — anagrams, cat game, guess the word — 20 a day between them
  • Thinking games — quiz, koto, geo, movie names — 30 a day between them

Some games pay double at certain hours, and your page always says which. The bonus counts towards the same daily limit, so it never lets anyone earn more than anyone else — it just gets you there in half the rounds. That is deliberate: it is a kindness to people with less time.
